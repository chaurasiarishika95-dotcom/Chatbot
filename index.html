How it works: your question is turned into an embedding, matched against 35 document chunks by cosine similarity, and the best matches are sent to Gemini as context. If nothing matches well enough, the bot says so instead of guessing. Open "How this answer was found" under any answer to see the chunks, scores, tokens, cost and time.
